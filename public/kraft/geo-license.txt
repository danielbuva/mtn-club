Candidate visible-surface vectors in geo-surface-candidates.json:
US public domain, derived from native pixels of the same NAIP raster.
Ten tentative partial surface traces have low spatial, boundary and named
association confidence. Shadows/dark lobes are omitted; physical identities
remain unresolved and complete bases unobserved. These are incomplete visible
surfaces, not complete boulder footprints or surveyed base boundaries.

Source requests, returned bounds, source IDs and file checksums are retained
in docs/kraft-gauntlet/source-data/geo-provenance.json.

Map colors are editorial. Neutral points locate source catalog records;
numbered clusters count catalog records, not physical rocks. Source area
centroids and cluster centers are not surveyed boulder positions. No physical
rock footprints are asserted. Geographic observations are not field verified
and do not constitute a verified final boulder approach.
