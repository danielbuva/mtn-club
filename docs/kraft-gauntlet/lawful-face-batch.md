## Capture and promotion handoff

For each unit, take an approach/identity frame, then a separate straight-on frame
for every climbing surface, with the ground/start holds, both side boundaries,
finishing lip and usable topout visible. Add oblique side/under-roof evidence
where a single view hides depth. Use an ordinary lens with enough setback;
retain uncropped originals and avoid stitched panoramas as the sole geometry
evidence. Use unobstructed views and legible light; record occlusion explicitly
instead of inferring covered holds.

Record photographer, capture date, original-file SHA-256, ownership/permission
and allowed adaptations/distribution, camera position/heading with uncertainty,
provisional physical-rock ID, face direction, and route IDs supported by the
view. Start/finish confirmation should come from field identification or
documented independent reference evidence. Original pixels and this metadata
first belong in ignored scratch.

Review the resulting **5–10 named physical rocks as one independent face batch**:
identity/boundaries, camera orientation, real feature correspondence, base/lip
visibility and reuse terms. Only critic-accepted photographs enter a concrete
promotion list with face IDs, attribution and unresolved limitations. Route
overlays require their own evidence and batch review afterward. The current four
held photos do not meet that gate, so there is no premature asset promotion or
individual UX critic loop.
