# Kraft source and production policy

Reconcile Mountain Project, OpenBeta and TheTopo at full Kraft scope. Snell
Press/Southern Nevada Bouldering III and KAYA are additional factual/editorial
references where legitimately accessible. Record unavailable access explicitly.
The [active tracker](TODO.md) and [content inventory](kraft-content-inventory.csv)
carry the work; no source is automatically canonical.

For every area, rock, face and climb, preserve source identifier/URL, source
name, source parent, grade and coordinate observations, retrieval date,
confidence and discrepancies. Select a canonical value only when evidence
supports it. Do not average grades or coordinates, collapse same-name variants,
or interpret database parent membership as proof of physical rock membership.
Describe route facts in original concise language. A corridor may communicate
a documented line without inventing exact holds.

Production face imagery requires self-shot ownership, documented permission,
public-domain status, compatible open licensing, or an approved independently
composed reconstruction. Mountain Project, TheTopo, KAYA and guidebook photos
and topo artwork are reference-only unless separate reuse rights exist.
OpenBeta content licensing must be checked separately from photograph rights.
The retained OpenBeta non-photo content has CC0 evidence; its MP import lineage
is correlated evidence, not independent confirmation. TheTopo's retained index
is research-only pending acquisition-policy/downstream-use review under its
[published terms](https://thetopo.com/site/terms). Automated acquisition stopped
when that constraint was found; no source pixels, topo points or runtime imports
are promoted from that index.
The [Pearl rights record](pearl-photograph.md) and
[geographic provenance](source-data/geo-provenance.json) preserve the existing
asset grants and hashes.

Independent reconstruction follows the [dedicated workstream](reconstruction-workstream.md):
synthesize factual geometry from independent references, choose a guide camera,
render an original scene, verify physical features and composition independently,
then author source-supported SVG routes separately. Preserve unresolved geometry
and stop promotion when evidence is insufficient. Keep reference-only pixels out
of the offline package and production asset directories.

Every record ends as COMPLETE, PARTIAL, BLOCKED on lawful imagery, BLOCKED on
route geometry/evidence, BLOCKED on identity/conflict, or EXCLUDED with a reason.
An unavailable asset never justifies silently omitting a known record. A source
snapshot, metadata validator or synthetic renderer test cannot supply real
physical or rights evidence.

Fresh critics inspect route facts, face accuracy, map placement/shape, UI field
use, offline/resilience and integration independently. Preserve one current
summary per domain; store ephemeral screenshots, experiments and research under
ignored `.tmp/kraft-gauntlet/`. Test the actual expanded package after denying
network transport and reopening the browser. Earlier four-rock passes are
baseline evidence only.
