# Current independent reconstruction review

Updated 2026-10-01. **No reconstructed guide image is approved.**

Monkey Bar remains blocked: six factual capture groups and 18 feature claims
support exterior research, but do not establish the required cave/roof depth,
complete starts/finishes and wraparound geometry. Its feature ledger and source
provenance remain in the Monkey record; reference-only photographs are unshipped.

An original movable-camera Cube east-face scene was built from multiple actual
photographs, with independently chosen front and oblique cameras. A separate
fresh geometry critic inspected actual clean renders and required major-plane,
rail and upper-terrain corrections. After those corrections, both views still
failed guide-image readiness: the modeled rock and finish were not reliably
recognizable without the title and source photographs. The shelves and connected
planes remained too generic. Photographs were materially more useful.

The Cube scene and renders stay in ignored scratch. This technique has not yet
proved a useful replacement for owned or permissioned field photography. Further
reconstruction requires stronger geometric fidelity, rather than texture or
cosmetic edits. No copyrighted photograph, framing or topo artwork was copied
into a production asset. Visual composition independence is not a legal guarantee.

The [reconstruction workflow](reconstruction-workstream.md) and provenance schema
remain available for later candidates. Human clean-image geometry review and
publication review remain unperformed; field verification is separately tracked.
The normal approved BLM Pearl photograph now has two independently reviewed
moderate corridors; it does not certify reconstruction or full-guide completion.
