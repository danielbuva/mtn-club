# Current independent reconstruction review

Updated 2026-10-01. **Pearl's second realistic raster passed independent image
review and a separate rendered mobile/desktop topo review.** The first raster
was rejected for retaining the reference's three-quarter camera. The accepted
image makes the main face nearly frontal and the right flank narrower, preserving
the recognizable shoulder, silhouette, seam and attached ramp.

Six actual photographs informed the factual geometry comparison. Generator pixel
inputs were limited to the public-domain BLM image and our own first render;
copyrighted photographs remained factual-only and unshipped. The optimized
original raster and independent SVG corridors are integrated. The asset record
preserves the exact prompt, camera, references, hashes and review limits. Human
publication review and field verification remain pending; small surface detail
does not establish exact holds.

The next bounded batch includes Split north/uphill, Potato Chip north/front and
Warm-up Main south/southwest. Three independent photographs support each
candidate's observed region. Split's first raster failed chimney width, base-rock
position and relative summit height. Its third raster preserves the corrected
macro geometry in ordinary cloud-filtered daylight. Warm-up Main’s second
raster retains its crown, oblique lower break, curved right corner and rocky foot
with subdued, uneven field light. A different critic inspected the actual latest
rasters against three real captures each and accepted scoped geometry and casual
field-photo character. Both are now in catalog 8; rendered-guide acceptance is
separate. Neither has an authored route line or reviewed hand placement.

Split’s image is limited to the Front Side Crack chimney context. The Mole and
Slice N Dice retain missing imagery. Warm-up’s shared main-plane image supports
seven source-qualified memberships in three views; four remain relative and
provisional. The complete east face, back and companion rock are outside scope.
All fine surface detail remains illustrative rather than surveyed holds.

Both Potato Chip attempts were rejected. The second improved the thin parallel
flaked lip and everyday light, but still inflated the distinctive tilted slab
into a rounded loaf. Realistic texture did not fix the physical form. The face
stays image-missing and field photography is preferable to forcing this method.

The latest standard is a useful casually observed crag photograph, with ordinary
conditions and slight imperfection: natural weathering, patchy tones, worn edges,
matte sandstone, restrained daylight and believable ground context. Commercial
hero lighting, sterile symmetry, uniform texture and isolated-object polish fail.

Monkey Bar remains blocked: six factual capture groups and 18 feature claims
support exterior research, but do not establish the required cave/roof depth,
complete starts/finishes and wraparound geometry. Its feature ledger and source
provenance remain in the Monkey record; reference-only photographs are unshipped.

An original movable-camera Cube east-face scene was built from multiple actual
photographs, with independently chosen front and oblique cameras. A separate
fresh geometry critic inspected actual clean renders and required major-plane,
rail and upper-terrain corrections. After those corrections, both views still
failed guide-image readiness: the modeled rock and finish were not reliably
recognizable without the title and source photographs. The shelves and connected
planes remained too generic. Photographs were materially more useful.

Cube's first realistic raster was also rejected: photographic appearance did
not compensate for incorrect squat proportions, a deep basin and enlarged
terraces. Both studies remain scratch-only. Owned or permissioned photography
is preferable to forcing those depictions. Visual composition independence is
not a legal guarantee.

The [reconstruction workflow](reconstruction-workstream.md) and provenance schema
remain available for later candidates. Human clean-image geometry review and
publication review remain unperformed; field verification is separately tracked.
Apply Pearl's actual-image standard in meaningful face batches. Reject generic
or geometrically inaccurate images. Never ship vector/primitive rock imagery or
reference-only photographs as reconstructed face assets. The current image and
two corridors do not certify full-guide content completion.
