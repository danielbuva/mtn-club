# Kraft open blockers

Current state, 2026-10-01. The goal continues; neither the old pilot nor the new
source inventory is a finished full Kraft guide.

- **Coverage integration:** source inventory extends to 370 current MP routes,
  245 OpenBeta routes and 198 separately indexed TheTopo research routes. Runtime
  still has four units / 58 climbs. Complete the reconciled runtime hierarchy,
  discovery and offline package without concealing blocked entries.
- **Physical identities:** source groups are not always one rock. Art Deco and
  Warm-up Boulders Main explicitly cover multiple rocks. Preserve 13 unmatched
  OpenBeta route IDs, legacy parents and five exact route parent conflicts.
- **Maps:** old west bound omits much of West Cluster; all four current outlines
  are proxies. Compare all source observations and aerial landmarks, reproject
  terrain consistently, author supported footprints and obtain a fresh map critic.
- **Images:** Pearl southeast has a lawful source-matched context image; its
  exact topo correspondence is unreviewed. Remaining views need legal photos or
  independently reconstructed, human-approved images. The rights CSV allocates
  every known MP/OpenBeta unit and TheTopo view for assessment.
- **Monkey reconstruction:** six distinct photographs have now been visually
  inspected. Cave/roof depth, complete starts/finishes/wraps, bearings/scale and
  current Direct geometry remain unresolved. No original model/camera/render
  exists; no asset approval is implied. Obtain missing overlapping geometry or
  choose field photography if reconstruction would be less useful.
- **Route corridors:** no real SVG currently ships. Reconcile actual face,
  starts, finishes, variants, eliminates and wraps independently of images.
  General supported corridors are allowed; unsupported exact beta is withheld.
- **TheTopo policy:** retained inventory is research-only pending source-use
  review. No further automated acquisition or runtime promotion; prefer lawful
  open/independent evidence for final records. See [provenance](source-data/thetopo-provenance.json).
- **Editorial access:** the SNB III book and paid KAYA guide have not been
  inspected. Public product pages cannot stand in for their climbing data.
- **UI / offline acceptance:** mobile image/list coordination is being repaired
  after fresh criticism. Re-test the built edition with transport denied, then
  obtain fresh field-use, offline and integration verdicts. Physical device
  Safari/installation remains unverified.

These gaps have specific evidence requirements; they are not grounds to invent
photos, map shapes, holds or topo lines, nor a reason to stop the other workstreams.
